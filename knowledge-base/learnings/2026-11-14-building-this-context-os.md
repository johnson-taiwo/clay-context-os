# Learning - building the Clay Context OS (14 Nov 2026)

**What worked**
- Starting from Bolaji's starter kit (skills + playbooks) meant the ICP and signal files follow the same format the
  skills read. `/signal-matrix` and `/campaign-copy` can run on them without changes.
- Reading the company's own homepage first gave verbatim positioning and approved-sounding proof in minutes.

**What was harder than expected**
- **Named prospects.** Public job boards hide the hiring company. The honest answer was "Requires Clay research with
  filters" plus lookalike seeds from Clay's own customer logos. No invented company names.
- **Pricing.** Clay's pricing page disagrees with itself ($167 vs $185 for Launch). Rule: never quote prices in copy;
  mark them UNVERIFIED in research.
- **Funding totals** differ by source ($215M / $372M / $392M). Keep them as ranges, out of copy.
