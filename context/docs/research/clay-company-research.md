# Clay - company research (public sources)

**Read:** 14 Nov 2026 by Johnson Taiwo. Public sources only. Where sources disagree, both figures are shown.
Re-check prices and funding before using them anywhere outside this repo.

## What Clay sells

Homepage (clay.com, read 14 Nov 2026):
- Headline: **"Build systems to grow revenue"**
- Subheadline: **"Infrastructure to get any data, run agentic workflows, and launch GTM plays."**

| Area (homepage wording) | What it means |
|---|---|
| Data | "a marketplace of data vendors under one contract", combining first- and third-party data. 200+ data and AI vendors, 150+ premium data sources |
| Agents | Claygent AI research agents: research, scoring and personalised outreach using context about your business |
| Orchestration | Build plays visually with clicks, code or chat (Sculptor workflow builder, reusable functions) |
| Activation | Send to email (Clay Sequencer), ads (LinkedIn, Meta, Google audience sync) and the CRM |
| GTM infrastructure | Reps self-serve prospecting data; ops runs central workflows |

Named features on the site: Claygent, Sculptor, Signals (job changes, web intent, webhooks), Audiences, ad sync, email
sequencer, CRM enrichment, TAM sourcing, waterfall enrichment, account and lead scoring.
Also: a Claude Code / agent plugin (`github.com/clay-run/agent-plugins`), Clay University, Clay Clubs (community
chapters, e.g. Lagos), and a Clay Experts directory of partner agencies.

## Pricing (clay.com/pricing, read 14 Nov 2026)

Clay changed its pricing in March 2026: credits split into **data credits** and **actions**.

| Plan | Headline price | Starts with | Notable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 500 actions + 100 data credits / month, 200 rows per table | Waterfalls, Claygent, bring your own API key |
| Launch | $167/mo | 15,000 actions + 3K data credits / month | Phone enrichment, Audiences search, job-change signals, sending email |
| Growth (recommended) | $446/mo | 40,000 actions + 6K data credits / month | CRM sync, HTTP API, webhook + web intent signals, ad audiences, Sequencer domains |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | SSO, SCIM, RBAC, credit budgeting, data warehouse sync, dedicated strategist |

**Conflict on the page itself:** the FAQ lists Launch at $185/mo and Growth at $495/mo. Treat prices as UNVERIFIED until
checked with Clay.

## Market around it

- Most-cited alternatives: Apollo (cheaper all-in-one), ZoomInfo (enterprise data depth). Workflow-layer rivals: Cargo,
  Unify. Persana was absorbed by Rox in 2026 (sources disagree on the exact month). Detail: `context/positioning/competitors.md`.
- The **GTM Engineer** job title is spreading: open roles were posted in Oct-Nov 2026 (e.g. a Staff GTM Engineer role via
  Jobgether, US remote, $139K-$166K base). Clay popularised the title.
- Clay Experts directory: about 186-189 partner agencies with tiers (Elite Studio, Studio, Advanced Artisan, Artisan).
  Counts differ by source.
