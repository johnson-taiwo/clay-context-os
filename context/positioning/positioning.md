# Positioning - Clay

*Built from Clay's public site (14 Nov 2026). The analysis is mine; Clay's own words are in quotes.*

## Category

Clay positions itself above "data provider": **GTM infrastructure**, meaning "Infrastructure to get any data, run
agentic workflows, and launch GTM plays." It is the layer between data sources and the tools that act on data (CRM,
sequencer, ads).

## Positioning statement (my draft)

For **B2B go-to-market teams that build their own plays**, Clay is the **GTM infrastructure** that **puts 150+ data
sources, AI research agents and activation in one workspace**. Unlike **a single contact database (Apollo, ZoomInfo)**,
Clay **combines many providers in a waterfall and lets teams build any workflow on top**, so coverage is higher and
plays ship without engineering tickets.

## Value proposition, by persona

| Persona | Value | Proof (homepage) |
|---|---|---|
| GTM engineer / RevOps | Every data source and AI step in one table; build plays with clicks, code or chat | Anthropic: enrichment rate 3x; Vanta: 80%+ coverage |
| VP Sales / CRO | More meetings per rep, less research time | Rippling 2x demos; Verkada 3x replies; Pendo reps at 200% of quota |
| CFO / procurement | One contract instead of many vendors; credit budgeting; SSO | "a marketplace of data vendors under one contract"; Enterprise controls |
| Founder | Start free; real plays without hiring a team | Free plan: 500 actions + 100 data credits / month |
| Agency founder | One production line across clients | Experts directory; reusable functions |

## What makes Clay different (the three things to say)

1. **Coverage through waterfalls.** Many providers queried in order beat one database. (Anthropic: 3x enrichment rate.)
2. **Build anything.** Tables + AI agents + HTTP + formulas + workflows. Not a fixed product: a builder's platform.
3. **Community and talent.** Clay made "GTM engineer" a job title. Clay University, Clay Clubs and the Experts
   directory mean hiring and help are easy to find.

## Weak spots to respect (don't pretend they don't exist)

- **Learning curve.** Builders love it; teams with no builder struggle. Hence the "No builder" row in the signal matrix.
- **Cost predictability.** Credits and actions confuse buyers. The March 2026 pricing change and the price mismatch on
  the pricing page add to it.
- **Not a sequencer first.** Clay now has a Sequencer, but most teams still pair it with Instantly, Outreach or Salesloft.
