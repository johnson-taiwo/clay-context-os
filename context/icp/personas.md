# Buyer personas - Clay

*Who decides, who builds, who signs. At a 30-person startup that is one person; at 2,000 staff it is four.*

## Who matters, by company size

| Company size | Decides | Builds (the champion) | Signs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under 50 | Founder / CEO | Founder or first growth hire | Founder |
| 50 - 500 | VP Sales / Head of Growth / CRO | RevOps lead or GTM engineer | CRO / CFO for annual plans |
| 500+ | VP RevOps / CRO | GTM engineering team, Marketing Ops | CFO + procurement + security (SSO, DPA) |
| Agency | Agency founder | Lead Clay builder | Founder |

## 1. GTM Engineer / RevOps lead (champion, the builder)

- **Titles:** GTM Engineer, Growth Engineer, RevOps Manager / Lead, Sales Ops, Marketing Ops, Growth Ops.
- **Their job:** build and run the systems that turn data into pipeline: lists, enrichment, scoring, routing, CRM hygiene.
- **What hurts:** five data vendors with five contracts; manual CSV work; reps asking for lists; enrichment holes.
- **What they want:** one place to wire sources together, test fast and ship plays without engineering tickets.
- **How they judge Clay:** coverage (waterfall hit rate), flexibility (HTTP, formulas, AI columns), cost per row.
- **Where they hang out:** Clay Slack and Clay Clubs, LinkedIn GTM-engineering posts, Clay University, YouTube.
- **Language:** "waterfall", "play", "table", "credits", "coverage", "signal".

## 2. VP Sales / CRO / Head of Growth (decides)

- **Titles:** CRO, VP Sales, Head of Sales, Head of Growth, VP Demand Gen.
- **Their job:** hit the number. Pipeline coverage, reply rates, rep productivity.
- **What hurts:** reps spend hours researching instead of selling; outbound reply rates falling; data cost rising.
- **What they want:** more qualified meetings per rep, proof inside a quarter.
- **Proof that moves them:** Rippling 2x demos, Verkada 3x reply rate, Pendo reps at 200% of quota, Canva 4 hours per
  rep per week (homepage results; see `context/messaging/facts.md` for allowed wording).

## 3. Founder / CEO (decides and builds, under 50 staff)

- **Their job:** find the first repeatable channel.
- **What hurts:** no team, no data budget, founder-led sales does not scale.
- **What they want:** a cheap start (Free / Launch plan), templates, a partner agency if they won't build it themselves.
- **Watch out:** low build time = churn risk. Point them to templates or an Expert agency.

## 4. CFO / Procurement (signs, 500+ staff)

- **Their job:** fewer vendors, predictable spend, security.
- **What they care about:** replacing several data contracts with one; credit budgeting; SSO / SCIM; DPA.
- **Proof:** "a marketplace of data vendors under one contract" (homepage); Enterprise plan controls.

## 5. Agency founder (decides; segment C)

- **Their job:** deliver lists and meetings for clients at a margin.
- **What hurts:** cost per lead, data quality complaints, builder time per client.
- **What they want:** reusable templates across clients, lower credit cost per row, the Experts directory listing.

## Never target

HR, talent, finance staff below CFO, engineers outside GTM, interns, students. They do not buy GTM tools.
