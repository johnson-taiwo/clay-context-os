# Tone of voice - Clay

*Read from Clay's public site, docs and community (14 Nov 2026). Logos and colours: use Clay's official brand page.
None are stored here.*

## How Clay sounds

- **Builder to builder.** It talks to the person who builds the system, not to a "decision maker".
- **Confident, plain, a bit playful.** "Build systems to grow revenue." Short claims, no fluff.
- **Shows instead of tells.** Templates, case-study numbers, live builds, University lessons.
- **Community-first.** Credits the people who build on it: Experts, Clubs, creators.

## Key phrases (Clay's own)

"GTM plays" · "agentic workflows" · "waterfall enrichment" · "build systems" · "GTM engineering" · "Claygent"

## Do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Name the play ("hiring signal", "job-change play") | Vague promises ("supercharge your sales") |
| Use real numbers from `context/messaging/facts.md` | Invent stats or round them up |
| Write for a smart operator | Explain what a CRM is |
| Short sentences, lowercase subjects | Buzzword stacks, exclamation marks |
