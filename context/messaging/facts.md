## Proof caveats

- Terrapinn is the only services-company proof. Don't use tech-company results to promise outcomes to services firms.
- Pendo "200% of quota" and Hex "+50% close rate" are on the homepage but left out: they are easy to over-read.

## Banned

### Banned angles
- Price or discount framing in cold email.
- Guarantees ("you'll get X meetings").
- "Replace your team" / "replace your SDRs".
- Attacking Apollo, ZoomInfo or any competitor by name.
- Anything that diagnoses the prospect's team as failing.

### Banned words (company-specific; global list in `knowledge-base/playbooks/copy-bans.yaml`)
- "cheap", "cheapest", "unlimited data"
- "scrape" (say "research" or "enrich")

## Voice notes

- Register: direct, curious, builder-to-builder. Short.
- Hedges about the prospect: "my guess is", "could be off".
- Subjects: lowercase, 2-4 words, no variables.

## CTA ladder

- **Primary:** "Worth a look?" / "Want the template?"
- **Secondary (final email):** "Who owns your data stack?"
- Never: calendar links in email 1.

## Internal only (never in prospect copy)

Pricing, revenue, valuation, customer counts, campaign results.
